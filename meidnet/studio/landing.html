MEIDNet Prism

Learn, build and benchmark multimodal AI for materials discovery

From the concepts of multimodal learning to a trained model and reproducible results. MEIDNet, the reference implementation, learns one space shared by crystal structures and their properties, and searches it for materials with target properties.
